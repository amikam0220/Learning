- **Definition(P 78)**
	An exemplification essay is an essay that use ==relevant and concrete examples== to support and illustrate the writer's point of view or opinion on a subject or topic. It is an approach to ==show==.

	The sentence that contains main idea of a paragraph is the topic sentence.
