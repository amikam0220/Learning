#### What is cohesion and coherence

cohesion:
	the degree to which sentences or paragraphs are connected so that the flow of ideas is easy to follow(==at the language level==)
coherence:
	the overall understandability of what we write or say.(==at the logic level==)

#### For Cohesion

##### Grammatical

- reference:
	determiners, pronouns, comparative words
- substitution:
	elements using the ones, the same, so, no, do/does/did, etc.
- ellipsis
	omission of words
- conjunction
	the use of transitional words or phrases that link sentences and paragraphs smoothly.

##### Lexical

- Reiteration
	words families, synonyms and antonyms
	hyponyms and superordinate
- collocation
	lexical chains
