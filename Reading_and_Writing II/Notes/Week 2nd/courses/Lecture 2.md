## **Unit 1 Language and Identity--Introduction to Expository Writing**

- About Exposition:
	Expose: to discover, reveal and make something clear.
	- Definition:
		Writing that exposes facts, explaining something or some ideas by providing related information.
	- Feature:
		**Objectivity**
	- Attentions:
		- Consider the readers
		- In a neutral way
		- Choose the appropriate medium
	- Parts:
		- The introduction
			- relevant info
			- the topic(the thesis statement)
		- The body
			- Topic sentence and specific evidence for each paragraph.
		- The conclusion
			- objective summary or suggestions
- Differences among narrative, expository and argumentative ^84efbe
	- narrative:
		To tell a story. For recording some events. With emotional feelings.
	- expository:
		For explaining the items.
	- argumentative:
		For convincing others. With intensively emotional feelings.
- Reading:
	- a strong focus
	- relevant information provided in an efficient manner
	- clear and accurate explanations
