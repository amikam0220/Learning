# Uncertainty in Measurements

A kind uncertainty :$u_{A}$
The component that can be processed by ==statistical methods== when multiple measurements are made with equal precision.
$$
u_{A} = t \cdot \sqrt{ \frac{\left( \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_{i}-\bar{x} \right)^{2}}{n(n-1)} }
$$
where $t$ is related to ==the number of measurements and confidence probability==.

B kind uncertainty: $u_{B}$
Components for a single measurement
The portion produced by ==measurer's estimate== $u_{B1}$, Maximum tolerance of the ==instrument== $u_{B 2}$; 
$$
u_{B}=\sqrt{ u_{B 1}^{2}+u_{B 2}^{2} }
$$

$$
u = \sqrt{ u_{A}^{2}+u_{B}^{2} }
$$
