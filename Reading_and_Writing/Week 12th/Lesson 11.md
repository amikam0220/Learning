# Unit 2 The Primary Purpose of Education--How to Make a Good Claim

## What is a claim

A ==claim== is the statement of one's position on a particular issue and appears as a statement of fact, definition, value or policy.

A ==counterclaim== is a claim set up ==in opposition to== a previous claim.

## Types of Claim

### Objective Claim

#### Claims of Fact

Something is true or not true.

### Subjective Claim

#### Claims of Values

Something is good or bad, more or less desirable.

#### Claims of Policy

One course of action is superior to another.
