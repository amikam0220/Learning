# Unit 5 Environment and Development--Cause and Effect

## Definition

Cause is ==why== something happens.
Effect is ==what== happens as a ==result== of a cause.

## Two Types of Cause

- immediate cause(==readily apparent==)
- ultimate cause(==not so apparent==)

## Criteria

- The cause happen ==before== the effort.
- Two things are ==related== in the first place.
- The effect can only be explained by the cause.

## Fallacies

- Confusing Cause and Effect
- Post hoc fallacy(Accepting A is the cause of B simply because A occurs before B)
- Ignoring a common cause

## How to Develop Support(For Body)

- In time order(chronological order)
- In space order(spatial order)
- In emphatic order(save-the-best-for-last)
