# Unit 4 Relationship--Classification and Division

## Classification-division Essay

It is a type of text structure that involves organizing information in which writers sort items into categories according to commonalities.

### Classification and Division (P 103)

Classification means the act or process of ==sorting out== ideas, objects or people into various types that are whole and independent. (Commonality)

Division refers to the act or process of ==breaking down== the ideas, objects or people into the smaller groups that are interdependent. (Difference)

###  Key Steps (P 103)

- Establish the ==single== ==principle==, which helps us sort things logically and systematically. 
- Determine the categories.
- Exemplify each category.
