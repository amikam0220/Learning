# Unit 2 Writing for Self-Discovery--Process Analysis

## What is Process Analysis

- **Definition:**
	Process analysis explains how something is made or done. It is a ==systematic review== of all steps and procedures followed to perform a given activity. It usually records an event in ==chronological== order.
- **Categories:**
	- Directional process (to tell how to do it)
		Clarify the steps in the procedure so that the readers can recreate the steps.
	- Informative process (to tell what it is)
		Explains how something happens or happened so that readers can gain a better understanding of the nature of that particular process.
	- Example (activity 5 essay 2 & 3)

## How to Write a Process Analysis Essay

- **For Introduction**
	- Grabber (hook)
		Draw readers into the essay.
	- Background Information (as needed)
		Any basic information the reader needs to know about the topic to understand in the essay.
	- Thesis Statement
		This is the most important sentence of your entire essay. It introduces the main point of the essay. Generally will appear in the first paragraph.
		And it tells two things:
		- an essay topic;
		- the writer's opinions(facts) about the topic.
		- Guidelines for writing a good thesis:
			- Make sure it is neither too broad nor too narrow.
			- Make the thesis statement debatable and contestable.
			- Assert only one main idea.
			- Define your idea in clear, specific terms.
