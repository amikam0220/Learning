		for mitigating the force with which propositions are expressed or refraining from the commitment.(vague language, cautious language or tentative language and with softening modifiers)

## Hedging

### What is hedging

An expression used to allow for ==additional possibilities== or to ==avoid over precise commitment==.(often, usually)

### Why use hedging language

Present the ==true state== of the writer's understanding and may be used to negotiate an ==accurate representation== of the state of the knowledge under discussion.

Render the statement ==less vulnerable to opposition==.

==Appear humble==.
