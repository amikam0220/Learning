# Unit 7 Ambition and Success--Definition

## Definition
## Purposes

- To clarify an abstract or controversial term or concept.
- To explain an unfamiliar term from a particular culture, geographical area, filed of study or profession.
- To inform and entertain a particular interested group of readers by giving various kinds of information about a term or concept.

## Categories

### Sentence Definition

#### Formal Sentence Definition

Term + Verb(be) + General Class + Distinguish features

#### Commonly Used Sentence Strategies

- with a dash(--)
- with a subordinate clause
- an appositive phrase
- "define as" "refer to" "consist in"

### Extended Definition

Consist of several sentences or several paragraphs. Usually offer an ==original perspective==.

#### Strategies

- Describe the ==features== and ==parts== of the subject, or the ==class== the subject belongs to
- Give examples
- Compare and contrast
- Trace the subject's history or development
- Analyze the subject's causes or effects
- Discuss the subject's etymology or origin

### Avoid

- unnecessary dictionary definitions
- circular definition
