# Integrals in Polar Coordinates

The double integrals in polar coordinates are: $$
\iint_{R}f(r,\theta)dA=\int_{\theta=\alpha}^{\theta=\beta} \int_{r=g_{1}(\theta)}^{r=g_{2}(\theta)} f(r,\theta)r \, dr  \, d\theta 
$$
It can be obtained by the Riemann sum of small pieces of area.

# Finding Limits of Integration

## Area in Polar Coordinates

The ==area== of a closed and bounded region $R$ in the polar coordinates plane is $$
A=\iint_{R}r\,drd\theta
$$
Obtain by taking the function as 1.

# Changing Cartesian Integral into Polar Integrals

$$
\iint_{R}f(x,y)\,dx\,dy=\iint_{G}f(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta)r \,dr\,d\theta
$$
