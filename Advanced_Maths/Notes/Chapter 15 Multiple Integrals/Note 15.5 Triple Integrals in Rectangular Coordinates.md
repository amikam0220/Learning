# Triple Integrals


$$
\iiint_{D}F(x,y,z)\,dV=\iiint_{D}F(x,y,z)\,dx\,dy\,dz
$$

We can see the function as the ==density of unit volume== and the integral as the sum of small ==mass==.

# Volume of a Region in Space

## Definition

The ==volume== of a closed, bounded region $D$ in space is 
$$
V=\iiint_{D}\,dV=\iiint_{D}dx\,dy\,dz
$$

# Average Value of a Function in Space

The average value of $F$ over $D$ is :

$$
\mathrm{ave}=\frac{1}{D}\iiint_{D}F\,dV
$$
