# Areas of Bounded Regions in the Plane

## Definition 

The ==area== of a closed, bounded plane region $R$ is 
$$
A=\iint_{R}dA
$$

It can be obtained by take $f(x,y)=1$ and the Riemann sum turns to the sum of areas.

# Average Value

## Definition

==Average value== of $f$ over $R$:

$$
\mathrm{Ave}=\frac{1}{A}\iint_{R}f\,dA
$$
