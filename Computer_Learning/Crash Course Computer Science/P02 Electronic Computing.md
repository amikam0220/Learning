# Electronic Computing

## Important part--Mechanical Relay

control wire uses ==electromagnetic field== to control the wire (like faucet and its handle)

### Limit

The speed is not fast enough and it exists wear and other problems.
## Alternative--Thermionic Valve(热电子管->真空管)

House two electrodes inside an vacuum glass bulb, where one electrode was heated to emit the electrons(==thermionic emission==) and another electrode ==with positive charge== attracts the electrons to form the flow. 

==Diode== is the component that permits only ==one-way== flow.

The third =="control" electrode== sits between the two electrons makes ==turn on and off the flow==.

### Advantages

More fast and less wear.
Turn the computers from ==electro-mechanical computing== to ==electronic computing==.

### Flaw

Easy to break down and expensive at the beginning.

## Reformation--Transistor

Two electrodes separated by ==semiconductor(半导体)==, which is controlled by the gate electrode.

### Advantages

More fast and small.
